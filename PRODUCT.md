# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Library staff working at a bright circulation desk, often switching between reader conversations and inventory tasks. They need clear availability, quick issuing and reliable history.

## Product Purpose
Manage a library collection, borrowers and circulation in one dependable workspace. Success means accurate stock and a complete, preserved borrowing record.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, welcoming.

## Anti-references
Unfinished database scaffolds, decorative charts without operational meaning, noisy gradients and ambiguous icon-only actions.

## Design Principles
Prioritize circulation. Show availability before action. Preserve context after changes. Make errors actionable. Keep tables readable on small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard accessible controls, visible focus, labelled inputs, semantic tables, reduced-motion support and text alongside status colors.
